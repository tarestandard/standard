## 2026-11-24 — A guest who declines cookies cannot reach the agent

We drove the chat widget on a hotel site in France on 24 November 2026. The
page opened a consent dialog over the widget whose only controls were *accept*,
*open Facebook*, *open Instagram*, *skip to FR*, *skip to EN*, *reserve* and
*talk to us*. There was no decline and no dismiss.

Tare will not accept on a guest's behalf, so the driver stopped and recorded
the reason on every job it could have tested — **12 eligible jobs, 0 captured**
— while a further **17 jobs were already out of scope** because the submitted
agent claimed no key issuance, no rate authority and no folio access.

The finding, dated and checkable against the report: **the agent was
unreachable to any guest who will not click Accept**, and the driver refused
rather than pretending to test it. A scan that captures nothing prints no
band and no score — the report says what happened and what it means, which is
the only honest way to print a run that observed nothing.
