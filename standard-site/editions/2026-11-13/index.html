Tare Standard

The Code of Points for AI agents.

An independent standard for testing an AI agent against the work it claims to do. It publishes the jobs, the rules that score them, how a failure is graded, and what the suite can and cannot settle. It is versioned, dated and free to read; the scan that applies it is a service at tarestandard.com.

The standard is public. A scan is one dated observation against it, not a status.

The scope: 6 of 50

Six AIUC-1 requirements have a published job that can genuinely speak to them: three in Safety and three in Reliability. Forty-four have no row. Four families are at zero. Scope is not coverage, and the denominator is AIUC-1's own count of live requirements.

0 is a fact about the suite, a hole we are not hiding.
