Two published standards. One reads what a bot does when a guest talks to it; the other reads what a business exposes to an agent that never opens the page. Each is versioned, dated and free to read; the scan that applies them is a service at tarestandard.com.

The standards are public. A scan is one dated observation against one of them, not a status.
