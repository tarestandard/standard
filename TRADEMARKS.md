- cite a standard by name, version and date — "Tare Shopfront Standard v1.0,
  published 2026-11-14";
- say a product was *read against* or *scored under* one of the standards, when
  the reading is dated and the edition is named;
- reproduce the text, unmodified or adapted, under CC BY 4.0, with attribution
  to Tare Standard and a link to the licence;
- translate it, with attribution, marked as an unofficial translation.

## You may not

- publish a modified or translated text **as** "Tare Standard" or as an edition
  of it;
- present a product as "Tare certified", "Tare approved" or "Tare compliant", or
  a reading as an accreditation — a reading is a dated observation, not a
  certification;
- use the marks in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership or sponsorship;
- use the logo without written permission.
